Cozy Space Furniture — 23 top-down pixel art props on a 16 px grid

Beds, desks, consoles, plants, lockers, a vending machine, a cryo pod and
more for space station interiors. Same 3/4 top-down view and palette as
Cozy Space Interiors (floors and walls) and Cozy Space Tiny Crew (16 x 16
characters).
